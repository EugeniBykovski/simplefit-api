# 07 — Legal and jurisdiction checklist

**This is a list of questions, not legal advice.** Completing it doesn't make anything
compliant. Nothing here has had legal review. Law named below is what an engineer thinks
counsel should look at, for counsel to confirm or correct.

**Starting point:** `docs/legal` holds only SF-71 drafts about Coach–Fighter data sharing,
all pending legal review. None covers public gym profiles. The production legal registry is empty.

| # | Item | Question for counsel | Mandatory before activation (proposed) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Business identity | What must be shown about a listed business, and by whom? | Yes | Not started |
| 2 | Legal entity verification | Is `sf103-generic-1` (legal name, country, one document) enough in each market? It has no per-country rules | Yes | Not started |
| 3 | Jurisdiction eligibility | Which countries first? Poland is a candidate only. What changes per country? | Yes | Not started |
| 4 | Published business address | A sole trader's address can be a home, and so personal data. Lawful basis, opt-in wording? | Yes | Not started |
| 5 | Business contact disclosure | Same for a phone number or email. Do traders have to publish contact details? | Yes | Not started |
| 6 | Privacy notice | Must be updated for public profiles, reports and search indexing. GDPR roles of SimpleFit and the gym? | Yes | Not started |
| 7 | User-generated content | Does hosting gym text make SimpleFit a hosting or online platform under the EU Digital Services Act? What duties follow, such as notice and action and statements of reasons? | Yes | Not started |
| 8 | Image rights | Copyright in logos and photos; who warrants them? Not needed for release 1, which has no images | Before images | Not started |

| 20 | Language | Which languages must legal text and notices be in? | Yes | Not started |

## Requires external legal review

Items 1–7, 10–13, 15–17 and 20 for the first release. Items 8, 9, 14 and 18 before the feature
that needs them.

## Requires a business decision first

* Who is the legal entity operating the service, and in which country?
* Who staffs moderation and receives legal notices?
* Which markets, in what order?
